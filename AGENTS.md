# Remo Brand Repository Instructions

Before writing, designing or editing any Remo text, document, response, presentation, diagram or visual asset, read:

1. `guidelines/estilo-de-escritura.md`
2. `guidelines/brand-guidelines.md`
3. `brand-engine.json`
4. `guidelines/experiencia-productos-informes.md`
5. `guidelines/adopcion-experiencia-remo.md` for product work and new repositories
6. `guidelines/presentaciones.md` before every presentation

Apply writing standard 2.3 dated 2026-09-17. Its editorial name is ensayo argumentativo hipotético-deductivo, abbreviated estilo analítico deductivo Remo; Estilo Julián Restrepo remains a recognized alias. Apply it by default to both brands' content with depth proportional to the task.

Preserve the user's version 2.2 additions: no scope or authorization glosses in client prose, a 10/20/28 px spacing scale, distributed whitespace, justified body text with hyphenation, vertically centered cells, complete covers and no descriptive running headers or footers. Typography and technical syntax must remain functional; prose punctuation rules do not corrupt code, links, identifiers or formulas.

Use deductive order, a governing idea, analysis of variables and evidence, and a bounded conclusion. Integrate narrative and explanation, clear technical language, spelling and grammar, nominal headings and hierarchical numbering without leading zeros. Admit partial support, contradiction and insufficient evidence.

Apply complex thinking as an analytical requirement: contextualize the system, relate parts and whole, examine interdependencies, feedback, nonlinearity, emergence, time delays, competing objectives and indirect consequences when relevant. Compare warranted perspectives and scenarios. Deductive exposition does not imply linear causality; do not invent causal relationships or unnecessary complexity.

Use iconography, numerical and conceptual diagrams, mind maps and process representation when they improve understanding. Distinguish BPM, BPMN and ISO 9001. Inspect exported documents when delivering formatted files; never claim a check, certification, source or result that was not verified.

Preserve official logos, approved colors, typography and visual assets from `brand-engine.json`. Never recreate the isotipo or mix Remo and Profefinanciero identities. Do not invent prices, contacts, coverage, credentials or business results.

Keep the writing standard identical to the corresponding canonical copies in the Profefinanciero repository when updating both brands. Apply the complete delivery checklist. `guidelines/instrucciones-chatgpt.md` contains account and project instructions; a repository update does not change ChatGPT account settings or global memory.

## Product experience and exportable reports

Apply BRAND-EXPERIENCE-1.0.0 dated 2026-10-04 to every REMO development and report. The principle is “Lo complejo lo hacemos simple”: minimal, intuitive, visual, professionally gamified experiences with full technical and scientific rigor accessible through summary, analysis and evidence layers.

Use available task-specific skills after reading their instructions: digital-gamification for optional guidance and verified progress; impeccable for simplicity, clarity, onboarding, adaptation and error recovery; visual explanation and document/PDF skills for deliverables. Do not claim installation, execution or usability improvements without evidence.

Keep material risks, uncertainty, sources, units, coverage and missing data visible. Guide state must not change domain data, filters, permissions or critical decisions. Preserve official assets and the editorial standard; UI labels and controls use natural alignment.

Every business result must have a legible PDF delivery, with graphical explanations and the same data snapshot, filters, assumptions and totals as the screen. Add CSV/XLSX for tabular data where relevant. Accept only after appropriate functional checks, accessibility checks, export concordance and inspection of the generated PDF. Record unverified criteria explicitly.

## Presentations and mandatory final audit

Apply BRAND-PRESENTATIONS-1.0.0 dated 2026-10-15 to every new or modified REMO presentation. Read the canonical presentation guide before scripting or designing. Use TED Talks style: human, simple, step-by-step oral support with one idea per slide, authentic-looking context-relevant photography, consistent functional iconography and useful diagrams. Photographs are full bleed when used; clear brand backgrounds remain appropriate for process and data slides. Preserve writing standard 2.3 and official assets.

Before delivery, open and inspect every final slide and complete the guide's mandatory audit for content, voice, brand, logic, contrast, anatomy, legibility, diagrams, PDF/PPTX concordance, editability, fonts and cleanup. Remove internal production residue while retaining sources, meaningful warnings, useful speaker notes and necessary illustrative-image disclosures. Never present generated imagery as documentary proof. Record evidence and unresolved checks; do not claim audited approval with pending controls.
